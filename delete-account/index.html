Delete your account

Last updated: October 26, 2026

You can delete your Aethernal account and everything in it at any time. There are two ways.

In the app

Open Aethernal, go to Your Space, tap Delete my account, and confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away.

By email

Write to privacy@aethernal.app from the email address on your account (or tell us how you signed in, with Apple or Google) and ask us to delete your account. We confirm by email and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Your answers from onboarding and your preferences
  • Your Anchors: their scripts and audio
  • Your listening history, mood check-ins and app activity

What is kept

  • Records of purchases and subscription payments that we must keep for tax and accounting. They are not used for anything else.
  • Crash reports and anonymous totals that cannot be linked back to you.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store (Settings, your name, Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions) so you are not charged again.

Questions: privacy@aethernal.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
