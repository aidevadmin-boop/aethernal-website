What is an Anchor?

An Anchor is the personal meditation Aethernal builds from what you tell it. The term comes from anchoring, a real technique for tying a feeling to a cue you can return to on purpose, not a marketing word invented to sound clever.

It's built once from your own bio-codified profile, your aspirations, patterns, and self-concept encoded into something the practice can actually speak back to you, then it stays yours to revisit, not a new one generated every time you open the app.
