Refund Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Billing is handled entirely by Apple and Google, not Aethernal

Every Aethernal subscription (Monthly, Yearly, and the 3-day free trial) is billed and processed through the app store you subscribed via, the Apple App Store or Google Play. Aethernal LLC never receives your payment details and cannot directly issue a refund, refunds, like the original charge, go through the store you paid.

How to request a refund

If you subscribed through the Apple App Store: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple ID you subscribed with, find the Aethernal charge, and follow Apple's refund request flow. Apple reviews and decides all refund requests according to its own policies, Aethernal cannot expedite, guarantee, or override Apple's decision.

If you subscribed through Google Play: go to play.google.com/store/account, find the Aethernal subscription under your purchase history, and follow Google's refund request flow, or visit support.google.com/googleplay for Google's refund policy and eligibility windows.

The 3-day free trial

The free trial requires a payment method on file but is not itself charged unless you continue past the trial period. To avoid being charged, cancel before the trial ends, from your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, not from within the Aethernal app, since cancellation is managed entirely by the store. Aethernal cannot reverse a charge that already occurred because a trial wasn't canceled in time; that determination is made by Apple/Google's own store policies.

The Free tier

The Free tier (fixed library only) requires no payment method and has nothing to refund.

If the app itself is at fault

If you were charged due to a verified bug or error on Aethernal's side (for example, a duplicate charge caused by an app malfunction, not by store billing behavior), contact billing@aethernal.app with your purchase details and we'll work with you directly, in addition to whatever the store itself offers.
